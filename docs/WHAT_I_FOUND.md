# SYZYGY — What I found

## A — Recommended

SYZYGY turns a sun→object→craft alignment into a one-thumb arcade timing read: you do not steer or shoot—you feel the syzygy arrive, then SNAP at the right instant. The auto-orbit makes each approach legible while the portal micro-bonus and brief LOCK freeze add texture without changing the core rhythm. It feels like catching a celestial beat, not driving a racer or aiming a shooter.

## B — Spare

The hook is timing a moving three-body line, not piloting through space: your craft auto-orbits, you read the sun→object→craft moment, and SNAP when the syzygy feels right. It is a compact one-thumb arcade loop with a portal micro-bonus and LOCK freeze as supporting rewards. The goal is to feel the alignment, not win a race or fire a shot.
