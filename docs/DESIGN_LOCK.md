# SYZYGY SNAP — design lock (arcade rebuild)

## Pitch
Arcade orbital alignment: aim through the dying sun and SNAP at the sweet spot.

## Loop
Drag aim → relic sweeps → alignment meter → SNAP (Perfect/Good/OK) → combo → waves. 3 lives.

## Progression
Wave N: faster sweep, tighter windows, shorter lifetime. 5 snaps per wave.

## Meta
Score, combo, local + global leaderboard. Pinch zoom. Catchy discreet futuristic pulse music.

## Aesthetic
Orrery / brass-obsidian / amber+cold — kept from contemplative build, now in service of arcade feel.
